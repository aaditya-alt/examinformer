RepeaterMock|SSC CGL 2025 (Held On: 12 Sept, 2025 Shift 1)

General Instructions:

  1. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer at the top right corner of screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself.

  2. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of screen will show the status of each question using one of the following symbols:

    • You have not visited the question yet.
    • You have not answered the question.
    • You have answered the question.
    • You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
    • You have answered the question, but marked it for review.

    The Mark For Review status simply indicates that you would like to look at that question again. If a question is answered but marked for review, the answer will be considered for evaluation.

Navigating to a Question:

  1. To answer a question, do the following:

    1. Click on the question number in the Question Palette to go to that question directly. This does NOT save your answer.
    2. Click Save & Next to save your answer and go to the next question.
    3. Click Mark for Review & Next to save, mark for review, and proceed.

You can view all the questions by clicking on the Question Paper button. This feature is provided so that you can see the entire question paper at a glance.

Answering a Question:

  1. Procedure for answering a multiple choice (MCQ) question:

    1. Choose one answer from options A, B, C, D and click the bubble.
    2. To deselect, click the bubble again or click Clear Response.
    3. To change your answer, click on another option.
    4. To save, you MUST click Save & Next.

  2. Procedure for answering a numerical answer type question:

    1. Enter a number using the virtual numerical keypad.
    2. A fraction (e.g. -0.3) can be entered with or without "0" before the decimal point. Up to four decimal places can be entered.
    3. Click Clear Response to clear your answer.
    4. To save, you MUST click Save & Next.
  3. Note that ONLY questions for which answers are saved or marked for review after answering will be considered for evaluation.
